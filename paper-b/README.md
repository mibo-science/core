# Paper B and the MIBO Pilot Core Repository

**Paper B:** *Operationalizing Longitudinal Machine Information Behavior: The Founding and Developmental Pilot of MIBO*

Paper B is a resource and methods paper. This repository contributes the historical conceptual, methodological, and claim-development context of the MIBO Pilot, including Longitudinal Machine Observation (LMO), the OPEN Principles, re-observability development, and Pilot claim governance. It does not contain the primary raw observation dataset or the complete Paper B manuscript.

## Evidence boundary

- Included period: Day 1–Day 13
- Included dates: 2026-05-05 through 2026-07-28
- Included observations: 244
- Collection: API-based continuously from Day 1

Day 13 / 244 is the Paper B evidence freeze, not the end of the MIBO Pilot. Later pre-2026-09-01 observations remain part of the developmental Pilot but are outside the Paper B evidence release.

## Related Pilot repositories

- [`mibo-research-pilot/queries`](https://github.com/mibo-research-pilot/queries) preserves the exact versioned Pilot stimuli and operational query instrument.
- [`mibo-research-pilot/reports`](https://github.com/mibo-research-pilot/reports) preserves observation evidence, corrections, provenance documentation, and the Paper B evidence manifest.
- [`mibo-research-pilot/core`](https://github.com/mibo-research-pilot/core) preserves historical conceptual, methodological, and claim-development context.

The historically named `core` repository is not the normative repository for the separate post-2026-09-01 MIBO Core program. Later-program protocols and architecture are outside its scope.
